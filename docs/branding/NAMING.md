# Intrik8 Yggdrasil — Naming and Brand Hierarchy

## Status

Current working brand guidance for the Yggdrasil project.

## Product Name

The formal product name is **Intrik8 Yggdrasil**.

The descriptive marketing form **Yggdrasil by Intrik8 Labs** may also be used.

The short internal and conversational name is **Yggdrasil**.

## Brand Hierarchy

```text
Intrik8 Labs
└── Intrik8 Yggdrasil
    ├── Týr
    ├── Mímir
    ├── Valhalla
    ├── Urd
    ├── Smidr
    └── other Yggdrasil modules
```

Intrik8 Labs is the company/organization brand. Intrik8 Yggdrasil is the product/platform brand. Norse-named modules are capabilities within the Yggdrasil platform rather than independent commercial products by default.

## Public Usage

Prefer **Intrik8 Yggdrasil** in:

- repository and project descriptions
- product websites
- release material
- commercial documentation
- presentations
- public-facing screenshots
- legal/trademark contexts

**Yggdrasil by Intrik8 Labs** is appropriate for descriptive branding and visual lockups.

Bare **Yggdrasil** is appropriate in source code, repository paths, internal documentation, and ordinary discussion when the context is unambiguous.

## Positioning

Yggdrasil is positioned as a modular business and operations platform spanning CRM-, ERP-, MSP-, work-management-, documentation-, asset-, automation-, monitoring-, and agent-oriented capabilities.

The product should not be positioned as an iGaming or gambling platform. Keeping the **Intrik8** source identifier prominent in public branding also helps distinguish the product from unrelated technology companies and products using the Yggdrasil name.

## Naming Rationale

Yggdrasil's tree metaphor reflects a common platform with interconnected branches. Individual modules provide distinct capabilities while remaining connected through explicit contracts and events.

The Norse naming system should support understanding and identity without replacing clear functional descriptions. Documentation should generally show both the module name and its responsibility, for example:

- **Mímir — Work Management**
- **Týr — Identity and Access**
- **Urd — Audit and Compliance**
- **Smidr — Agent Orchestration**

## Trademark Note

This document records product direction, not legal clearance. Formal trademark availability and registration strategy should be reviewed with qualified counsel before substantial commercial launch or branding investment.
